I saw a [post on reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/raspberry_pi/comments/46kvww/experimenting_with_the_new_raspbian_opengl_drivers/) of someone who had been experimenting with running the full version of Minecraft on a Raspberry Pi 2 using the new [Experimental Open GL drivers](https://www.raspberrypi.org/blog/another-new-raspbian-release/) and I thought I have to try that!

Note - the drivers are experimental, I wouldn't recommend doing this on your 'normal' sd card, get a new one and keep them separate or make sure you have a backup.

Get the latest version of [Raspbian](https://www.raspberrypi.org/downloads/raspbian/) which includes the experimental drivers (2016-02-09) and flash an SD card.
