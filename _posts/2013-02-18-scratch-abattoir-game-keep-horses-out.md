I've been trying my hand at [Scratch](http://scratch.mit.edu/), I wanted to see if I could transition myself as a traditional programmer to it. Learning how Scratch was structured was probably the biggest challenge, but once I put the structure in my own language (I now call sprites objects in my head), I got on pretty quickly.

![](http://scratch.mit.edu/static/projects/martinohanlon/3111490_med.png)I decided to write a simple game, I fancied something which was [topical](http://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-21335872) and, I realise now, being slightly sick, I came up with "Abattoir", the concept of the game is "You get points for getting cows in the mincer and loose a life for every horse that 'accidentally' gets in", if this doesn't celebrate the horse meat scandal I don't know what does.

**[Play Abattoir here](http://scratch.mit.edu/projects/martinohanlon/3111490)**

The controls are pretty simple, click the **green flag** to start, the red circle to stop and press **\[space\]** to open and close the mincer. Leave it open for the cows which fall off the conveyor and close it for the horses.
